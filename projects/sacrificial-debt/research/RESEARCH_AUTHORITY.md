# Research Authority

The authoritative substantive research design is the **Sacrificial Debt Research Program Prospectus v0.2 (20 August 2026)**.

The software must not silently revise its research question, concepts, hypotheses, falsification criteria, case roles, or evidentiary safeguards.

Central question:

> Under what conditions does perceived exemption from collective sacrifice become an unpayable political debt that legitimates the coercive suffering or death of the allegedly nonsacrificing group?

The workbench is designed to make it easier to discover that the theory is wrong, incomplete, or limited than to accidentally confirm it.
