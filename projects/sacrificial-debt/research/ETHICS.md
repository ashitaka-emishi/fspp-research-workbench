# Ethics and Historiographical Safeguards

- Do not treat persecuted/genocide victims as voluntary sacrificial participants.
- Do not collapse military sacrifice and genocide into one analytical category.
- Perpetrator rhetoric is historical evidence, not project moral endorsement.
- Sacrificial Debt is not a total explanation of the Holocaust.
- Preserve provenance/context around quotations.
- Include victim, survivor, and Jewish sources where the research question/source record permits.
- Label speculative inference explicitly.
- Keep historical-actor claims separate from researcher conclusions.
