# Provisional Mechanism

- **A — Sacrificial mobilization:** threat/mobilization, visible surrender, sacrifice as measure of virtue/membership.
- **B — Asymmetry and moral comparison:** lesser-sacrificing population, moralized inequality, attributed claim/debt.
- **C — Essentialization and political activation:** public debtor category, declining dischargeability, institutional collection.
- **D — Coercive equalization and closure:** imposed suffering framed as restorative/equalizing; extreme closure where continued existence is represented as incompatible with restoration.

This is a sequence to test, **not a universal law**. Cases may stall, branch, reverse, or fail to exhibit modules.
