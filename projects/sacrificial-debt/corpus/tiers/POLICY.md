# Corpus Tier Policy

## Tier 1 — Core interpretive
Fully annotated, provenance-complete, eligible for strongest interpretive/causal claims after review.

## Tier 2 — Validation
Matched/larger corpus for recurrence, robustness, negative findings, and generality. Lighter annotation permitted. Does not independently support strongest causal claims.

## Tier 3 — Search/reference
Candidate discovery, lexical search, chronology, context, negative searches. **A hit is a lead, not coded evidence.**

Promotion is explicit and recorded; never change a tier simply because an item is useful to a claim.
