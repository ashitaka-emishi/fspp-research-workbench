# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

This project expects careful, respectful collaboration. Contributors should make the work more rigorous, legible, and humane.

Examples of constructive behavior include:

- grounding claims in evidence and exact source coordinates;
- distinguishing uncertainty, inference, and established fact;
- treating historical violence and genocide material with care;
- welcoming correction without personalizing disagreement;
- preserving methodological boundaries even when expediency is tempting.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, bad-faith disruption, or careless treatment of victims, perpetrators, or vulnerable groups represented in the research record.

## Historical Sensitivity

The project includes Holocaust/genocide material. Contributors must not represent victims as voluntary sacrificial participants, reproduce perpetrator moral logic as project endorsement, or strip quotations from provenance and context.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, restrict participation, or take other action needed to protect the project and its contributors.

Report concerns through the repository security/contact channels or directly to the maintainers.
